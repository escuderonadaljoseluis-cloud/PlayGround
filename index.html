HOW TO FIGHT

🕹
MOVE

Drag the left stick. Or use WASD / arrow keys. On desktop, click-drag the arena too.

👊
PUNCH

Tap PUNCH (or J) three times to chain a combo. Third hit sends them flying.

🦵
KICK

Slower, longer reach, bigger knockback. Tap KICK or K. Great for closing space.

🛡
BLOCK

Hold BLOCK (or L) to eat 80% less damage. You still take chip damage — and gain meter.

★
SPECIAL

Fill the SPECIAL meter, then unleash your meme's signature move. Tap ★ or U.

🏆
WIN

Beat your rival in 2 of 3 rounds. K.O. or highest health when the clock hits zero.

Pro tip: block a punch, then immediately combo back. Works every time. (Not every time.)
